### Correlation / reuse rules verified
- One canonical provider fact/derived state is reused across relevant pages; pages do not independently recalculate competing values.
- New contextual evidence does not modify frozen deterministic Setup Score formulas.
- AI consumes the same canonical evidence objects rather than fetching an independent market truth.
- Stale/plan-limited/unavailable provider data remains labeled and cannot masquerade as current live evidence.
- Operational telemetry stays in Data Engine/Maintenance; desks receive decision-relevant results, not maintenance noise.

## UI preservation rule
Gate 4 passing does **not** mean making pages denser. The audit removes genuine dead space/duplication and corrects hierarchy while preserving readable charts, tables, typography and comfortable spacing. v14.1 compact redesign behavior remains rejected.
