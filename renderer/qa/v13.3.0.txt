Release intent
v13.3.0 consolidates the approved #1–#32 scope into a stable, horizon-specific market-intelligence workflow. It keeps Dashboard/Decision Queue concise, keeps Discovery as candidate generation, keeps Day/Swing/Long as specialist decision desks, keeps Research optional and evidence-oriented, and keeps AI advisory. Data freshness/cache operations are made safer and more visible without changing live quote freshness or the validated deterministic trading model.
