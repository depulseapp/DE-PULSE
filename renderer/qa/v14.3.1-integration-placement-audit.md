## Tab-by-tab placement audit

- **Dashboard:** existing hierarchy retained. Market Open and macro results feed existing Market Regime / Decision Queue / More Market Context; no new bottom panels.
- **Day:** actual entry-zone, liquidity, premarket reaction and event risk stay close to candidate/plan/readiness evidence.
- **Swing:** only meaningful checkpoint/catalyst context is surfaced; transient Day-only noise is intentionally hidden.
- **Long:** only material earnings/filing/macro/fundamental fallback context is surfaced; intraday noise is intentionally hidden.
- **Discovery:** provider intelligence does not replace Discovery Rank; contextual evidence is used after candidate qualification/staging where appropriate.
- **Research:** deeper earnings/fundamental/ownership evidence remains the primary home for sourced provider detail.
- **AI:** receives canonical evidence only; no independent fetch and no score authority.
- **Settings:** Save remains a persistent action row and preserves scroll/focus; no sidebar overlay may jitter during rerender.
- **Maintenance:** Provider Capability Registry, prep/catalyst operational states and source freshness remain here.
- **Documentation:** v14.3.1 completion behavior, cadence and limitations documented.

Result: **Integrated / placement approved**, subject to final responsive, functional, trader and frozen-source gates.
