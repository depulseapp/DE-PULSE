## Why this patch exists

The independent pre-v16.1 audit of v16.0.2 found three late truth defects: an old Fundamentals snapshot could be Fresh after a recent route check; Research could treat future quote timestamps differently from Provider Reconciliation; and incomplete corporate-action pagination could stamp a historical backfill as complete. v16.0.3 fixes those issues and is the first build to use the improved Working Candidate process so professional/adversarial defects are found before package stamping instead of through repeated public TEST patches.

## Closed scope

- **#16 Provider Reconciliation / quote timestamp truth — CLOSED.** Provider/data age and receipt age remain separate; materially future timestamps are invalid; Research shares timestamp validity while retaining its own documented freshness cadence.
- **#17 Corporate Actions / lifecycle — CLOSED.** Historical backfill completion requires natural pagination completion. Partial/truncated/error states are retryable and tested through recovery.
- **#25 Selected-Ticker Research Truth — CLOSED.** Fundamentals now require both underlying Data Age and selected-ticker Check Age. Invalid future evidence no longer displays as age-zero Fresh truth.
- **#30 Professional release governance — CLOSED.** Professional Expert, Extensive Edge, independent authorization, fault injection and reproducible randomized gates are permanent.
