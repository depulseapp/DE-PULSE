# DE.PULSE v16.9.0 — Performance / Data-Efficiency Baseline

v16.9 reuses existing owners and introduces no always-on community polling loop, second history engine, or futures feed. Community fusion is bounded to retained evidence (200 persisted items / 50 visible normalized items / 30 fused clusters). Replay scenario catalog is derived from already-loaded canonical history/events and capped at 20 descriptors. Heavy persistence remains v17.

Release certification must verify no material regression in existing 500-symbol, cache/concurrency/goroutine/stop-timeout/catalyst and source-health gates.
