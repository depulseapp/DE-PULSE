# DE.PULSE v17.5.0 — Professional Trader / Investor Real-Money Review

## Real-Money Review posture

This review treats v17.5 as a decision-support system used around real money. It does not assume a passing architecture review implies safe market behavior. v16.11 remains the authoritative Stable while v17 is under Major Closure qualification.

## Freshness and readiness truth

The inherited v16.11 acceptance remains blocking: a stale live-provider quote cannot remain Day READY, market-level `DATA DEGRADED` blocks clean readiness, wide/current liquidity becomes CAUTION, high-impact macro events lower readiness, and a closed session cannot look like a live-ready entry. These safety overlays do not mutate protected deterministic Day/Swing/Long scores.

## v17 runtime/load truth

v17 adds explicit workload priority, provider budgets, bounded queues, load shedding and reasoned degradation. Maintenance exposes Performance & Runtime Load, Warm Start, Recovery and Critical Decision Data usability so a partial degradation such as broad Discovery delay can be distinguished from selected/watchlist truth loss.

## OPEN-002 closure — stale evidence vs liquidity risk

The v17.4 correction is real-money material. Stale/missing/future-skewed quote evidence remains conservative data-quality/freshness risk, but it is no longer multiplied into a separate `LIQUIDITY RISK` for every symbol. Genuine current spread/liquidity risks remain visible, grouped by cause/count, and every symbol retains Review drill-down. Shared Quotes STALE truth remains visible once as the upstream root cause.

## Community / AI / adaptive safety

Community/Telegram-style evidence remains contextual/untrusted and does not alter deterministic scores. AI remains a second opinion after evidence hydration and cannot silently mutate Action/Score or execute. Adaptive promotion remains `SHADOW → VALIDATED → APPROVED → PRODUCTION`.

## No Execution Boundary

The executable HTTP route inventory contains research, maintenance, data, watchlist, AI, validation and app-lifecycle actions only. v17 introduces no order, broker-routing, execution, portfolio, journal or blotter endpoint. Opportunity Radar remains decision support only.

## Closure defect found and fixed

OPEN-002 proved that a truthful low-level conservative state can still become misleading when transformed at a downstream surface. The fix classifies stale evidence at the freshness layer and reserves per-symbol liquidity risk for current measured market evidence. That distinction is now covered by backend and renderer tests.

## Verdict

**PASS candidate for v17 Major Closure**, contingent on the complete independent certification matrix (deterministic equivalence, professional acceptance, HTTP/cross-module, race, performance and source/data hygiene). No real-money issue in this review justifies weakening a freshness/readiness guardrail or pulling execution scope into v17.
