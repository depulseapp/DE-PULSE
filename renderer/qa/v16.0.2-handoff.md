## Why this patch was required

The independent v16.0.1 Professional Expert Trader/Investor audit found that green engineering gates still missed decision-truth edge cases: absence of provider comparison could appear Fresh, a newly received stale market quote could participate in current reconciliation, corporate raw-history pagination/completeness was not proven, partial provider failures could be overwritten as healthy, and later renderer overrides could hide v16 Maintenance/AI truth. v16.0.2 closes those defects and makes their adversarial reproductions permanent release tests.

## Professional trader/investor acceptance findings

- **No evidence is not agreement.** Research cannot call Provider Reconciliation Fresh without current evidence.
- **Single source is explicitly partial.** It remains usable evidence but is not represented as independent cross-provider confirmation.
- **Market-data age is independent from receipt age.** A newly received old quote is excluded from current reconciliation.
- **Session-aware truth is preserved.** A valid recent prior close can remain appropriate on weekends/closed sessions while stale regular-session evidence is rejected.
- **Material provider conflicts remain blocking** for decision-grade selected-ticker Research.
- **Raw corporate history is no longer boolean truth.** Coverage now records state, requested range, first/last bar, bar count, page count and pagination completion.
- **Partial provider pagination stays degraded** until a complete recovery succeeds.
- **The final active Maintenance renderer exposes v16 truth**, and the final active AI context receives Research Package, Evidence Snapshot, Provider Reconciliation and Corporate Action truth.
- **Deterministic Day/Swing/Long Score/Action remains frozen.** 2403/2403 equivalence passes.
