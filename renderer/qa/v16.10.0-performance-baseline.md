# DE.PULSE v16.10.0 — Performance / Data-Efficiency Baseline

Opportunity Radar uses bounded batch snapshots, cached eligible-universe metadata, rotating candidates and at most five live-priority promotions. It never permanently streams the entire U.S. universe. Market Activity/news/community/canonical liquidity/history are reused rather than refetched by a second owner.

Release benchmark on the certification-class Linux host before freeze: 500-candidate promotion selection is approximately sub-millisecond and ~0.13 MB/op; 500-candidate full opportunity-metric enrichment is approximately 17 ms and ~13.2 MB/op. The blocking gate uses deliberately broad host-tolerant ceilings of 50 ms/20 MB and 150 ms/40 MB respectively, plus inherited cache/concurrency/goroutine/stop-timeout/catalyst/500-symbol protections.
