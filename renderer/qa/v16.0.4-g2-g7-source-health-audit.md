## Permanent architecture-fit rule

Before adding a capability, the G2 decision must explicitly identify what is **REUSED, CONSOLIDATED, REFACTORED, DELETED/REPLACED, and only then ADDED**. A new tab/module/provider path must not become a second owner of an existing canonical truth.

## Certification orchestration improvement

The v16.0.4 retrofit also fixes the release-process inefficiency exposed by repeated wrapper interruptions. `certification_runner.py` + `certification_plan.json` now checkpoint every expensive check by exact source fingerprint and classify outcomes as PASS, PRODUCT FAIL, INFRA FAIL or BLOCKED. Randomized seeds and race shards are independent processes with durable logs/results, and nested heavy reruns were removed from Approved Scope / Previous-Build gates. Professional authorization now aggregates checkpointed constituent checks rather than restarting the whole sequence after an interruption.

This is a permanent release-process rule: **do not rerun already-clean expensive certification work on an unchanged source fingerprint merely because a later check or the orchestration process was interrupted.**
